Need-aware Smart Match

The matching engine now considers four factors instead of simply selecting the nearest shelter.

📍
Distance — 40 points Nearby eligible shelters receive stronger routing scores.
📦
Capacity fit — 25 points Shelter must have enough capacity for the food donation.
🛡
Food safety — 15 points Delivery must remain inside the safe-use window.
❤️
Recipient need — 20 points Shelters reporting higher current need receive additional priority.

💡 How to use Need Level

High: food is urgently required.
Medium: normal current demand.
Low: shelter currently has adequate supply.

Updating this value affects future Smart Matches.
